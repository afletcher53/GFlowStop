# Held-out amortized Bayesian stopping results

This experiment is cost-sensitive Bayesian finite-horizon stopping with policy distillation, not a GFlowNet. The teacher solves the fitted beta-binomial remaining-count model exactly and hard certificates force acceptance once the yield threshold is reached and rejection once it is unattainable. The fitted model can still be wrong for real rankings.

Nested repeated stratified folds are stored in `splits.json`; intervals resample topics after averaging repeated folds, stochastic seeds, and the nine decision conditions. Direct net utility is primary. The full-information gap is only a labelled upper-bound comparison, not a hindsight stopping oracle.

The observed Amortized-count minus Count-Bellman mean utility is 0.00662, with bootstrap 95% CI [0.00120, 0.01253] and paired sign-flip p=0.025.
